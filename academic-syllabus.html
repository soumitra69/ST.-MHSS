MBOSE Prescribed Syllabus

» Class-Wise & Subject-Wise Syllabus

Secondary Section — MBOSE SSLC (Classes IX & X)Meghalaya Board of School Education (MBOSE) — SSLC Board Syllabus

English (Compulsory Core)

MBOSE prescribed prose, poetry, supplementary reader, unseen comprehension, grammar, and formal writing (100 Marks: 80 Written + 20 Internal).

Mathematics

Number systems, algebra, trigonometry, coordinate geometry, mensuration, statistics & probability (80 Written + 20 Internal).

Science & Technology (Theory + Practical)

Chemical substances, world of living, natural phenomena (light/electricity), and laboratory practical record & viva.

Social Science

India and the Contemporary World, Contemporary India (Geography), Democratic Politics (Civics), Economics & Disaster Management.

Khasi (MIL) / Alternative English & Health / Computer Education

MBOSE Khasi literature & grammar, and sixth subject elective (Computer Science / Health & Physical Education).

CCE & Terminal Evaluation

» Examination Pattern, Unit Test, Half-Yearly, Annual & Practical Schedules

Classes I to XII

First Unit Test / Periodic Assessment I

First Week of May (May 04 – May 12, 2026)

Pattern: 20 / 25 Marks (CCE Internal Component)

Pen-and-paper unit test covering April–May syllabus chapters and class assignments.

Nursery to Class XII

Half-Yearly / First Term Examination

First–Second Week of July (July 06 – July 16, 2026)

Pattern: 80 / 100 Marks Written + Practicals

Comprehensive mid-year terminal examination followed by Parent–Teacher Report Card Day.

Classes I to XII

Second Unit Test / Periodic Assessment II

Second Week of August (August 10 – August 14, 2026)

Pattern: 20 / 25 Marks (CCE Internal Component)

Second continuous evaluation cycle testing post-midterm syllabus and lab records.

Classes IX, X, XI & XII

Pre-Selection (X & XII) & Pre-Promotion (IX & XI)

Mid-September (September 14 – September 24, 2026)

Pattern: Full MBOSE Board Pattern (80/70 Theory + 20/30 Practical)

Mandatory qualifying preparatory examination modeled strictly on MBOSE SSLC & HSSLC blueprints.

Nursery to Class XII

Selection Exams (X & XII) & Annual Promotion Exams

Late November – Early December (Nov 23 – Dec 05, 2026)

Pattern: 100% Final Annual / Board Selection Qualifying

Final annual promotion exams for Nursery–IX & XI, and final MBOSE Selection clearance for X & XII.

Classes VIII to XII

Science, Computer & Geography Practical Examinations

June 22–26 (Mid-Term) & November 16–21 (Annual/Selection)

Pattern: 20 Marks (SSLC) / 30 Marks (HSSLC Labs)

Hands-on laboratory experiments, certified practical record book evaluation, and oral viva voce.
