Our Mission & Our Vision

Rooted in the pioneering Catholic educational heritage established in 1961 by Fr. Michael Balawan, St. Michael's Higher Secondary School, Umsning is dedicated to forming confident, competent, and compassionate “Michaelians” who strive ever upward (Excelsior) and step forward with courage (Shaphrang).

Institutional Mission
Our Mission • St. Michael's Umsning
Institutional Aim & MissionAim: Overall Development of the Child

» Our Mission

Guided by our supreme institutional aim — Overall Development of the Child — St. Michael's Higher Secondary School provides an inclusive, values-led education that forms every learner intellectually, morally, spiritually, culturally, and socially in the Catholic tradition from Nursery to Class XII, nurturing disciplined and compassionate citizens.

Through dedicated classroom mentorship, modern science and computer laboratories, synchronized physical drill, and pastoral care under the Catholic Mission of Umsning, we accompany each child to discover their God-given potential.

Intellectual & skill formation across Nursery to Class XII
Moral integrity, personal discipline & spiritual growth
Cultural appreciation, regional pride & social service
Torch of the MichaeliansMotto: Excelsior & Shaphrang

» Our Vision

Our vision is to empower every “Michaelian” to step forward with confidence, character, and competence under our motto Excelsior (Ever Upward) & Shaphrang (Step Forward) — nurturing responsible leaders who serve Meghalaya and the nation with excellence, cultural pride, and moral conviction.

We envision a learning community where rural and urban youth alike achieve state-level distinction in MBOSE examinations while remaining deeply rooted in humility, civic duty, and respect for all.

Confidence, academic competency & MBOSE excellence
Psychological resilience & character formation
Responsible citizenship & visionary leadership

From the Official School Diary

» Our Seven Core Educational Objectives

Holistic Formation • Nursery to Class XII
Objective 01

Intellectual Formation

Cultivating analytical curiosity, scientific inquiry, linguistic fluency in English and Khasi, and academic mastery across the MBOSE curriculum.

Objective 02

Skill Acquisition

Equipping learners with practical laboratory skills, computer literacy, public speaking, teamwork, and problem-solving capabilities for the modern world.

Objective 03

Appreciation of Culture

Honoring and preserving the indigenous Khasi and Ri Bhoi cultural heritage alongside national harmony, literature, and choral arts.

Objective 04

Moral Integrity

Instilling honesty, respect for parents and elders, truthfulness, and ethical responsibility in daily conduct inside and outside the campus.

Objective 05

Spiritual Formation

Nurturing reverence for God, daily morning prayer, inner peace, and universal brotherhood in the Catholic missionary tradition.

Objective 06

Psychological Growth

Providing a safe, encouraging, and joyful environment where every child builds self-esteem, emotional maturity, and resilience.

Objective 07

Social Responsibility

Forming compassionate citizens who care for the environment, serve rural communities, and stand up for justice and the common good.

Torch of Michaelians

“Excelsior” & “Shaphrang”

Every objective converges on a single sacred trust: forming young men and women of character, courage, and competence for Meghalaya and the world.
