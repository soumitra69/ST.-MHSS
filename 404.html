St. Michael's Higher Secondary School • Umsning

» Who We Are (General Information)

The School is under the management of the Catholic Mission of Umsning, Archdiocese of Shillong, and is one of the oldest co-educational institutions in the Ri Bhoi District of Meghalaya. It was started as a Lower Primary School in 1961 by the great Educationist and French Catholic Missionary, Fr. Michael Balawan.

The institution is backboned by its committed teachers who spark the imagination of their students and give wings to their dreams. The spirit of confidence and competency is the hallmark that “Michaelians” carry with them from the torch of their school's motto “Excelsior” & “Shaphrang”. The school runs classes from Nursery to Class XII with English as the medium of instruction.

» Academic Progression & Pathways

English medium education from Nursery to Class XII rooted in MBOSE academic standards, intellectual formation, and moral integrity.

STAGE 01
Nursery & Kindergarten (KG)

Early Years Foundation

Activity-based foundational literacy, numeracy, phonics, and joyful socialization in a warm, caring environment (8:45 AM – 2:00 PM).

STAGE 02
Classes I – V (Primary Section)

Lower & Upper Primary

English medium instruction, mathematics, environmental studies, weekly unit tests, and two annual Term Examinations (8:45 AM – 2:00 PM).

STAGE 03
Classes VI – X (MBOSE SSLC)

Secondary Section

English medium instruction with Half-Yearly/Pre-Selection and Selection/Promotion exams, laboratory practicals, and SSLC board mentoring (8:45 AM – 3:00 PM).

STAGE 04
Classes XI – XII (Science, Arts & Commerce)

Higher Secondary (HSSLC)

Pre-Promotion & Promotion exams (Class XI) and Pre-Selection & Selection exams (Class XII) with dedicated labs and fieldwork (8:45 AM – 3:00 PM).
