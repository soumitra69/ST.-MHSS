From the Desk of the Alumni Patron

» Welcome Home, Dear Michaelians

No matter how many years have passed since you last walked down our school corridors, stood in the morning assembly under the Umsning pine breeze, or ran on the school football grounds, you will forever remain an essential part of the St. Michael's legacy.

The Michaelian Alumni Association exists to nurture enduring bonds between past pupils, current students, and the faculty. Through guest mentorship lectures, scholarships for deserving scholars, career counseling sessions, and infrastructure endowments, our alumni continue to uplift the youth of Ri Bhoi District.

“Once a Michaelian, Always a Michaelian.” We welcome your return, your memories, and your continued stewardship.

65-Year Journey of Excellence

» Institutional Milestones of Our Alumni Body

1961

Founding Year by Fr. Michael Balawan

Established with humble thatched classrooms under the Catholic Mission to bring English-medium literacy to Umsning and the surrounding rural villages of Ri Bhoi.

1985

First MBOSE SSLC Cohort

Full recognition and affiliation under the Meghalaya Board of School Education, producing our first generation of teachers, public servants, and community leaders.

2005

Higher Secondary Upgrade

Introduction of Class XI & XII Arts, Science, and Commerce streams, opening collegiate career paths for local scholars right at home in Umsning.

2015

Golden Era & State Merit Ranks

Michaelians regularly secure top 20 state ranks in MBOSE examinations, winning Governor and Chief Minister felicitations and state science honors.

2026

8,500+ Strong Global Fraternity

Over 65 glorious years of academic rigor, moral integrity, and regional leadership spanning medicine, engineering, civil service, business, and education.

Green Umsning Afforestation & Watershed Care

Every year, alumni collaborate with the Eco Club to plant hundreds of indigenous pine and fruit saplings across Umsning and neighboring hill tracts to safeguard the natural watersheds of Ri Bhoi District.
