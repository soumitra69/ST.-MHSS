Who We Are (General Information)

St. Michael's Higher Secondary School • Catholic Mission of Umsning, Archdiocese of Shillong

The School is under the management of the Catholic Mission of Umsning, Archdiocese of Shillong, and is one of the oldest co-educational institutions in the Ri Bhoi District of Meghalaya. It was started as a Lower Primary School in 1961 by the great Educationist and French Catholic Missionary, Fr. Michael Balawan.

The institution is backboned by its committed teachers who spark the imagination of their students and give wings to their dreams. The spirit of confidence and competency is the hallmark that “Michaelians” carry with them from the torch of their school's motto “Excelsior” & “Shaphrang”. The school runs classes from Nursery to Class XII with English as the medium of instruction.

Motto: “Excelsior” & “Shaphrang” (Ever Upward · Step Forward with Courage)

65 Years of Educational Heritage (1961 – 2026)

» Our Journey Through the Decades

1961Pioneering Era

Foundation as a Lower Primary School

Started as a Lower Primary School in 1961 by the visionary French Salesian missionary and mathematician Fr. Michael Balawan under the Catholic Mission of Umsning, Archdiocese of Shillong, bringing formal schooling to rural Ri Bhoi.

1970s – 1990sAcademic Growth

Expansion to Upper Primary & Secondary Section (MBOSE SSLC)

Under the dedicated stewardship of Diocesan priests, Missionary Sisters, and lay educators, the school expanded into a full-fledged Secondary institution preparing candidates for the MBOSE SSLC Board Examinations with consistent 100% pass results.

2000s – PresentHigher Secondary Excellence

Upgradation to Higher Secondary (Arts, Science & Commerce)

Upgraded to St. Michael’s Higher Secondary School offering Class XI & XII streams in Science, Arts & Humanities, and Commerce with dedicated Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Computer laboratories, serving over 2,000 students.

2026 & Beyond65th Anniversary Milestone

65 Years of Legacy & State Top 20 Merit Distinction

Celebrating 65 years of holistic education with MBOSE SSLC State Top 20 Merit Rank honors felicitated by the Hon’ble Chief Minister of Meghalaya and backed by the ₹20,000 St. Michael’s SSLC Award Body Endowment.
