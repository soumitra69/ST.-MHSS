Residential Amenities

Hostel Facilities & Living Environment

Our girls' dormitory blocks are thoughtfully built with clean ventilation, personal study spaces, modern washroom blocks, and strong security protection, giving parents complete peace of mind.

Living Space

Comfortable Dormitories

Spacious, well-ventilated dormitory rooms with individual cots, storage cupboards, personal mattresses, and study desks for each resident.

Safety & Protection

24/7 Security & CCTV

Round-the-clock boundary surveillance, secure gated access, and trained resident female wardens on duty inside the residential premises at all times.

Dining & Kitchen

Hygienic Dining & Pure Meals

Wholesome, home-style meals prepared fresh three times daily in a dedicated sanitized dining hall under strict nutritional supervision.

Campus Infrastructure

Continuous Power Backup

Uninterrupted power supply via automatic institutional generator sets guaranteeing reliable lighting, ventilation, and study facilities 24/7.

Health & Hygiene

Treated Water & Clean Washrooms

Multi-stage filtered pure drinking water stations and sanitary, modern washrooms maintained daily with continuous running water supply.

Quiet Evening Prep

Supervised Study Hall

Dedicated quiet study hall with individual desk spaces, supervised every evening from 5:00 PM – 8:30 PM for disciplined academic preparation.

Medical Support

Infirmary & First-Aid Care

Immediate emergency medical support and first aid under an on-call attendant, with direct hospital escort to medical centers in Umsning when needed.

Spiritual Sanctuary

Chapel & Morning Devotion

Peaceful prayer space fostering spiritual grounding, daily reflective morning assembly, and Sunday worship services for resident students.

Sports & Wellness

Sports & Evening Recreation

Secure playground space for volleyball, badminton, indoor recreation, and guided social interactions during designated evening breaks.
